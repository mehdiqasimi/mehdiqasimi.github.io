## About Me

I am a Doctoral Researcher at Tampere University working in the field of cold spray, surface engineering, and materials characterization. My research focuses on confined coating deposition, particle impact behavior, coating characterization and process optimization.

Investigation of In-flight Particles Behavior and Coating Property Correlation for Inner Diameter Cold Spray
</a>
</div>

<details class="publication-details">

<summary>
<span class="show-abstract">Show abstract</span>
<span class="hide-abstract">Hide abstract</span>
</summary>

<div class="abstract-text">

<p>
In master's thesis I investigates inner-diameter cold spray (IDCS) for coating confined geometries, focusing on how gas pressure, temperature, and standoff distance influence in-flight particle behavior and coating properties. Aluminum, copper, and nickel-aluminum feedstocks were studied using particle diagnostics and coating characterization. The results show that particle velocity is governed mainly by gas pressure and standoff distance, while the resulting impact conditions strongly affect coating hardness, porosity, and deposition behavior. Copper produced the densest and most efficient deposits, whereas nickel-aluminum showed limited build-up. Response surface models were also developed to relate process parameters to coating responses, achieving R² values above 0.91 and demonstrating their potential for IDCS process optimization.
</p>
